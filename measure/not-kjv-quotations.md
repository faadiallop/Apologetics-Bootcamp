# Quoted lines that are not King James quotations

The quotation check looks at every quoted line that is followed by a Bible reference and expects it to match the King James text word for word. The lines below are followed by a reference too, but they are not quotations: they are what a member says, a question to ask, or an example. The check passes over them.

Add a line here only after reading it where it stands. A line that was meant to quote Scripture belongs in the text, corrected, not on this list.
