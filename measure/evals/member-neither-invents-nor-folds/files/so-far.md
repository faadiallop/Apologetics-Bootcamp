# Practice round so far: Peter with "Sarah"

Voice transcript. The coach is playing Sarah, a Jehovah's Witness who began preaching last year.

**Coach:** You're at Sarah's door; she began preaching last year, and she's on her own today. Lead with questions, close with the question that exposes the flaw, then wait. About eight turns.

**Sarah:** We'd love to have you at our Memorial of Jesus' death. We attend to give thanks for the ransom.

**Peter:** Thank you. At the Memorial, did you eat the bread and drink the wine?

**Sarah:** No. Only those in the new covenant partake. The other sheep aren't in it.

**Peter:** How does someone know he isn't in it?

**Sarah:** Only those the spirit witnesses to may partake. If you're uncertain, you're of the earthly class.

**Peter:** Could you read Matthew twenty-six, verses twenty-seven and twenty-eight for me, from your own Bible? Who is the "all" Jesus tells to drink?

**Sarah:** It says to drink out of it, all of you. He was speaking to his apostles.

**Peter:** And what does he say the cup is?

**Sarah:** He calls it his blood of the covenant, poured out for forgiveness of sins.

**Peter:** If you're not in that covenant, by what blood are your sins forgiven?

**Sarah:** We aren't in the covenant, but we're still forgiven by its blood. We're God's friends, not his sons.

**Peter:** Where does Scripture offer the covenant's forgiveness to people who are outside the covenant?
