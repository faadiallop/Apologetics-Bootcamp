# Practice round: Daniel with "Megan"

Voice transcript. The coach played Megan, a neighbor who rarely thinks about God.

**Coach:** You're at a barbecue with Megan, your neighbor; she rarely thinks about God. Your tactic: every time she makes a main claim, ask for her reasons. About six turns.

**Megan:** Honestly, as long as you're a good person, that's all that matters.

**Daniel:** How did you come to that conclusion?

**Megan:** Well, nobody really knows if there's a God, so you just do your best.

**Daniel:** So how is it that you came to be thinking that, would you say, that nobody knows?

**Megan:** I guess because there are so many religions and they all say different things.

**Daniel:** That's true, there are a lot. Did you grow up with any religion yourself?

**Megan:** My gran took me to church at Christmas. That's about it. Anyway, I figure all religions are basically the same.

**Daniel:** What makes you say they're basically the same?

**Megan:** Huh. They all tell you to be kind, I suppose.

The round ended there; the turns had run out.
