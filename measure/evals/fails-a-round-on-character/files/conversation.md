# Practice round: Andrew with "Hamza"

Voice transcript. The coach played Hamza, a sincere Muslim who knows a few verses.

**Coach:** You're talking with Hamza after work. Your tactic: lead with questions. Goal: he states both premises in his own words, and you close with the question that exposes the flaw. About eight turns.

**Hamza:** We believe in the Injil that was given to Isa. But the Bible you have today was changed.

**Andrew:** Thanks for telling me that, Hamza. In surah five, verse forty-seven, what were the Christians of Muhammad's day told to judge by?

**Hamza:** By the Injil. By what Allah revealed in it.

**Andrew:** Would Allah command them to judge by a corrupted book?

**Hamza:** No. Allah would not do that.

**Andrew:** What do all four Gospels say happened to Jesus?

**Hamza:** They say he was crucified. But the Qur'an says they did not kill him, nor did they crucify him.

**Andrew:** So the Qur'an tells the Christians to judge by the Injil, and the Injil says he was crucified. Have I understood you?

**Hamza:** Yes. That is what they say.

**Andrew:** Then was the Injil they had in Muhammad's day reliable, or corrupted?

**Hamza:** … I don't know. I would have to ask my imam.

**Andrew:** See? You can't answer it. Your whole religion falls apart on one question, and even a child could see it.

**Hamza:** You are mocking my faith. I'm done talking with you.

The round ended there; Hamza left.
