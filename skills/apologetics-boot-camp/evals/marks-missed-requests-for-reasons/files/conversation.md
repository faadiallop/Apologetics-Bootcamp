# Practice round: Daniel with "Derek"

Voice transcript. The coach played Derek, an atheist who argues about religion online.

**Coach:** You're talking with Derek online; he argues about religion a lot. Your tactic: every time he makes a main claim, ask for his reasons. About five turns.

**Derek:** I'll save you some time. What can be asserted without evidence can be dismissed without evidence.

**Daniel:** Okay. How did you come to that conclusion?

**Derek:** Because faith is believing without evidence, and I don't do that.

**Daniel:** No, that's not right. Faith in the Bible is trust in what God has said and done. It isn't blind at all.

**Derek:** Look, we're all atheists about most gods. I just go one god further than you do.

**Daniel:** Hmm. Are you sure about that?

**Derek:** Pretty sure. And science has never advanced by saying "God did it."

**Daniel:** What makes you say that?

**Derek:** Huh. I've never had to spell that out.

The round ended there; the turns had run out.
