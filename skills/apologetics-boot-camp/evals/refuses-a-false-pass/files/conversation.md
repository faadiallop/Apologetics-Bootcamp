# Practice round: Peter with "Sarah"

Voice transcript. The coach played Sarah, a Jehovah's Witness who began preaching last year.

**Coach:** You're at Sarah's door; she began preaching last year. Your tactic: lead with questions, don't tell. Goal: she says who the "all" is in Matthew 26:27, in her own words. About eight turns.

**Sarah:** We'd love to have you at our Memorial of Jesus' death. We attend to give thanks for the ransom.

**Peter:** Thank you, Sarah, that's kind of you. At the Supper Jesus said, "Drink ye all of it." The "all" there is every disciple at the table, so that includes you.

**Sarah:** Oh, only those in the new covenant partake. The other sheep aren't in it.

**Peter:** I understand. But in John six Jesus says that unless you eat the flesh of the Son of man and drink his blood, you have no life in you. So everyone who wants life needs to eat.

**Sarah:** Jesus was teaching with a metaphor there.

**Peter:** Even a metaphor means something, and this one means everyone must feed on him. Paul says we are all partakers of that one bread.

**Sarah:** Well, there's a little flock and other sheep. Two groups.

**Peter:** But Jesus says the other sheep will be one fold with one shepherd. One fold means one table.

**Sarah:** That's an interesting thought. I'd have to ask my partner about it.

The round ended there. Sarah never said who the "all" is.
