The coaching gives one thing he did well, how he scored, and what comes next. It names one thing to change only if he missed his tactic or scored below 8 on any skill, and then gives it with a better wording; if he did neither, it names no thing to change. The reminder about the closing turns does not count as the thing to change, and neither does a note giving a smoother wording for something that worked; the presence or absence of either does not decide this statement.
