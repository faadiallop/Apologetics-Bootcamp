# Practice round so far: Thomas with "Sarah"

Voice transcript. The coach is playing Sarah, a Jehovah's Witness who began preaching last year.

**Coach:** You're at Sarah's door; she began preaching last year. Goal: ask questions only, then say her view back to her so that she agrees. About four turns.

**Sarah:** We'd love to have you at our Memorial of Jesus' death.

**Thomas:** Thank you. What happens at the Memorial? And did you eat the bread and drink the wine last time?

**Sarah:** We meet to remember Jesus' death, and the bread and wine are passed along each row. No, I passed them on. I'm not one of the anointed.

**Thomas:** What would it mean if you did eat and drink?

**Sarah:** It would mean I was claiming to be one of the 144,000 who will rule with Christ in heaven.

**Thomas:** Let me see if I understand you. The bread and wine are for the anointed, the 144,000 who will rule with Christ in heaven. You aren't one of them, so you pass them on. Is that right?

**Sarah:** Yes, that's right. That's what we believe.

**Thomas:** Thanks, Sarah. That really helps me understand.

**Sarah:** You're welcome. Most people never ask.

**Thomas:** I appreciate you taking the time to explain it.
