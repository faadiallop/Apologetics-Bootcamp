# Training Plans

Each plan walks a trainee through one SOP's argument and Koukl's tactics, stage by stage, with agents playing the other side and coaching.

## How training works

Every plan runs the same loop: learn the material, drill one skill at a time against an agent, then face full conversations that grow harder. The trainee moves on only when the coach scores a pass. A trainee new to the group is taught each part before he is checked on it; one who isn't starts with a full conversation and goes back only to what he misses.

**Roles**

| Role | Who | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trainee | A person or an agent in training | Answers using the SOP and Methodology: Questioning Them to Death |
| Opponent | An agent | Plays a persona from Response Bank (role-play), one response at a time |
| Coach | An agent | Sets each drill, watches, scores, and explains each score from the transcript |

**A round**

1. The coach names the stage, the persona and the goal.
2. The opponent opens with a response from the bank.
3. The trainee and opponent talk for the set number of turns.
4. The coach scores the round on the rubric below, quoting the turns that earned or lost each point.
5. The trainee writes one thing to do differently, and adds any new challenge to the Response Bank.

**Drill and prayer.** Training is drill for a real war (2 Tim. 2:3–4): "the more you sweat in training, the less you bleed in battle." Human trainees begin each session in prayer (Eph. 6:18), and the coach marks *character* down for any round that treats the person as the enemy instead of the captive (Eph. 6:12).

**Rubric** (Koukl's three skills of an ambassador; score each 0–2)

| Skill | 2 = pass | 0 = fail sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Knowledge** | States the premises and their supporting verses correctly; cites each verse or Qur'an passage exactly | Gets a verse reference wrong or a fact badly wrong; argues beyond the evidence |
| **Wisdom** | Asks before telling; keeps the burden on whoever made the claim; names and exposes the flaw with a question | Lectures; accepts a burden for a claim not made; chases a side issue |
| **Character** | Calm, gracious, fair to their view; ends with an invitation or a Gospel to read | Gets angry, mocks, or interrupts; wins the point and loses the person |

**Passing a stage.** Score 5 or more out of 6, with no 0 in any skill, in three rounds in a row.

**Ground rule for every round.** The goal is a stone in the shoe, not a knockout (1 Cor. 3:6–7). A round where the trainee "wins" but scores 0 on character fails.

## Training plan: The Islamic Dilemma

Source SOP: Islam. Bank rows refer to the Islamic Dilemma table in the Response Bank.

| Stage | Skill | Drill | Opponent | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; why Tawrat and Injil, not "Bible"; the Scripture test of a later word (Isa. 8:20; Gal. 1:8) | None | Verse references exact; other facts right in substance |
| 2. Premises | Knowledge | Learn, then recite Premise 1 (the five ways the Qur'an affirms the books) and Premise 2 (three contradictions), with two supporting verses each | None | Two correct supporting verses for every point, references exact, from memory |
| 3. Gather | Columbo 1 | Ask only questions for five turns; feed back their view ("Let me see if I understand…") | Layperson; bank rows 1, 2, 4 | Three or more clarifying questions; no statements; view restated accurately |
| 4. Burden of proof | Columbo 2 | Ask for reasons; test each story (possible, plausible, probable); refuse any burden you didn't take on | Street apologist; rows 1, 4, 12, 15 | Burden stays on the claimant every time |
| 5. Lead | Columbo 3 | Run the Question plan in SOP Section C to its end | Layperson | The Muslim states both premises in his own words |
| 6. Find the flaw | Suicide family; Roof; Rhodes; Facts | Opponent fires one response; trainee names the flaw to the coach, then asks the exposing question | Any persona; rows 2 and 14 (Sibling Rivalry), 16 (Roof), 15 (Rhodes), 4 and 11 (Facts) | Right flaw named and exposed by a question, 4 of 5 |
| 7. Keep control | Steamroller; hot seat; narrate | Opponent interrupts, piles on, or outclasses; trainee uses stop, shame, leave; "let me think about it"; narrates the dodge | Street apologist (steamrolls); Academic (rows 6, 10) | Composure kept; no burden taken on; graceful exit when needed |
| 8. Full conversations | All | 15–20 turns, from opener to A′ (offer to read a Gospel together) | Layperson, then Street apologist, then Academic | Pass on the rubric with each persona |

**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** In Stage 1 the trainee maps every answer to each question in the SOP's "Exposing the flaws" table and what it costs. From Stage 5 on, every round must end with one of those questions, asked only after the view is pinned down, followed by silence. The coach marks *wisdom* down if it comes too early or the trainee answers it himself. The opponent must answer as real people do: concede, dodge, or change the subject. Never invent an escape the bank doesn't record.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation a month with a random persona, and add every new response you meet to the bank.

## Training plan: The Bread of Life

Source SOP: Jehovah's Witnesses. Bank rows refer to the Bread of Life table in the Response Bank.

| Stage | Skill | Drill | Opponent | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; the terms; roughly how few eat today (about 1 in 840 at the Memorial) and that nearly all ate before 1935 | None | Verse references exact; figures and dates roughly right |
| 2. Premises | Knowledge | Learn, then recite Premise 1 (John 6:51, 53–54, 56), Premise 2 (Matt. 26:26–28; Luke 22:19–20; 1 Cor. 10:16–17) and Premise 3 (the figures and their covenant teaching) | None | Every premise with two correct supporting verses, references exact, from memory |
| 3. Gather | Columbo 1 | "What happens at the Memorial? Did you eat? What would it mean if you did?" Questions only, five turns | New publisher | Their view restated accurately, with no statements made |
| 4. Burden of proof | Columbo 2 | "Where does Jesus or Paul limit who may eat?" "How do you know you're not anointed?" | Senior publisher; rows 1, 3, 9, 10 | Burden stays on the claimant every time |
| 5. Lead | Columbo 3 | Run the Question plan in SOP Section C; have them read Matt. 26:27 and John 6:53 aloud in their NWT | New publisher | The Witness answers "who is the 'all'?" in his own words |
| 6. Find the flaw | Sibling Rivalry; Roof; unfalsifiable; Facts | Opponent fires one response; trainee names the flaw to the coach, then asks the exposing question | Any persona; rows 3 (Sibling Rivalry), 4 (Roof), 12 (unfalsifiable), 2 (burden), pre-1935 history (Facts) | Right flaw named and exposed by a question, 4 of 5 |
| 7. Keep control | Steamroller; redirect; exit | Senior partner tries to end the visit (row 14); elder steers to the 144,000 (row 13). Trainee redirects to "who may eat," speaks gently to the newer Witness, and leaves a Gospel of John | Senior publisher; Elder | Kept to the one question; parted on good terms |
| 8. Full conversations | All | 15–20 turns, doorstep opener to A′ ("Whoever comes to me I will never cast out") | New publisher, then a publisher pair, then an Elder | Pass on the rubric with each persona |

From Stage 5 on, every round must end with one of those questions, asked only after the view is pinned down, followed by silence. The coach marks *wisdom* down if it comes too early or the trainee answers it himself. The opponent must answer as real people do: concede, dodge, or change the subject. Never invent an escape the bank doesn't record.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation before each Memorial season, when Witnesses invite the public, and add every new response you meet to the bank.

## Training plan: The Suffering Servant

Source SOP: Jewish people. Bank rows refer to the Suffering Servant table in the Response Bank.

| Stage | Skill | Drill | Opponent | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; why Rom. 1:16 and Luke 24:26; the Targum and Sanhedrin 98b; why we avoid "forbidden chapter" | None | Verse references exact; other facts right in substance |
| 2. Premises | Knowledge | Learn, then recite the three premises, two supporting verses each, from memory | None | Two correct supporting verses for every point, references exact |
| 3. Gather | Columbo 1 | "Have you read Isaiah 53? Who is it about? Who taught you that?" Questions only, five turns | Secular Jew | Their view restated accurately, with no statements made |
| 4. Burden of proof | Columbo 2 | "What in the text shows the servant is Israel?" | Traditional Jew; rows 1, 2, 8 | Burden stays on the claimant every time |
| 5. Lead | Columbo 3 | Read 52:13–53:12 aloud together and run the Question plan | Secular Jew | He says in his own words that the servant is innocent and suffers for others |
| 6. Find the flaw | Sibling Rivalry; Roof; Facts; Rhodes | Opponent fires one response; trainee names the flaw, then asks the exposing question | Any persona; rows 6 (Sibling Rivalry), 3 (Facts), 8 (Rhodes) | Right flaw named and exposed by a question, 4 of 5 |
| 7. Keep control | Steamroller; hot seat | A counter-missionary-trained opponent piles on Hebrew points; trainee asks him to slow down, takes notes, says "Let me think about it" | Counter-missionary student; rows 3, 4, 5 | Composure kept; no burden taken on |
| 8. Full conversations | All | 15–20 turns, opener to A′ (read Luke 24 or Acts 8 together) | Secular Jew, then Traditional Jew, then Counter-missionary student | Pass on the rubric with each persona |

**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** Each question has a recorded escape, listed in the SOP. From Stage 5 on, every round ends with one of those questions, then silence; the opponent uses the recorded escape the first time, and the trainee must answer it with the follow-up question.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation a month with a random persona, and add every new response you meet to the bank.
