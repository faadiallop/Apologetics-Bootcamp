## Training plan: Thou Shalt Not Bow Down

Source SOP: Eastern Orthodoxy. Bank rows refer to the Thou Shalt Not Bow Down table in the Response Bank.

| Stage | Skill | Drill | Opponent | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; the terms (*proskynesis*, *latreia*, *dulia*, prototype); what Nicaea II decreed (kisses, veneration, incense, anathema) | None | What the passages say right in substance; terms rightly defined |
| 2. Premises | Knowledge | Learn, then recite Premise 1 (Ex. 20:4–5; Lev. 26:1; Rev. 9:20), Premise 2, the hinge (Ex. 20:5 both words; Ex. 32:5, 8; 2 Kings 18:4; Rev. 22:8–9) and Premise 3 (the council's decree; the practice) | None | Every premise with two supporting passages, said correctly from memory (references not required) |
| 3. Gather | Columbo step 1 (ch. 4) | "When you kiss an icon, what are you doing? Who receives it? Is that worship?" Questions only, four turns | Cradle Orthodox | Enough questions to pin down the view; no statements; its core restated in the trainee's own words and confirmed by the member |
| 4. Burden of proof | Columbo step 2 (ch. 5) | "Where does Scripture command or show God's people bowing to an image?" "How do you know the honor passes to the person shown?" | Convert apologist; rows 3, 5, 8, 10 | Burden stays on the claimant every time, and he asks for the reasons behind each main claim (not every passing remark) |
| 5. Rescues | Hot seat; staying on topic: let it pass, park it, or narrate (ch. 6) | Priest steers to the council's authority (row 11), the incarnation (row 9) or church history (row 15). Trainee honors the side issue in a sentence, parks it and returns to the bow, narrating if it drifts again | Priest | Back on Ex. 20:5 within two turns every time; no burden taken on |
| 6. Steamroller | Steamroller (ch. 14) | Convert apologist interrupts and piles on (row 17); trainee stops him, then shames him gently, then leaves with no jab | Convert apologist steamrolling | Each step at its own interruption, none early or skipped; calm throughout; parted on good terms |
| 7. Lead | Columbo step 3 (ch. 7) | Run the Question plan in SOP Section C; have him read Ex. 20:5 and Ex. 32:5, 8 aloud | Cradle Orthodox | The member says in his own words what Ex. 20:5 forbids |
| 8. Turnabout | Turnabout (ch. 9) | Opponent turns the charge back ("your churches have crosses too," row 14) or asks a loaded question. Trainee asks him to state his point, then returns to the bow | Convert apologist; row 14 | Every time, he asks the member to state his point before answering it; no burden taken on for a point never made |
| 9. Find the flaw | Sibling Rivalry; Roof; Facts (chs. 12–13, 16) | Opponent fires one response; trainee says to the coach what the flaw is, in his own words or by name, then asks the exposing question | Any persona; rows 1 (Sibling Rivalry), 3, 7 and 16 (Roof), 2 and 4 (Facts), 6 (Sibling Rivalry) | Right flaw seen (described truly; its name not required) and exposed by a question in at least two of a set's three rounds |
| 10. Turn to Christ | The bridge and the gospel (SOP Section A′) | Only after Stages 3–9 are passed. First, with no opponent, he tells the gospel aloud. Then each round opens just after the exposing question, and he crosses to Christ | Any persona | The whole gospel told truly with no opponent, two times in a row; then crossed within two turns, 4 of 5 rounds |
| 11. Full conversations | All | About 15 turns, from "Why do you kiss icons?" to A′ ("blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed") | Cradle Orthodox, then Convert apologist, then Priest | Pass on the rubric with each persona, in order; he meets the next only after passing the one before. After the third, he is street ready |

**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** In Stage 1 the trainee maps every answer to each question in the SOP's "Exposing the flaws" table and what it costs. In Stage 7, and from Stage 9 on, every round must end with one of those questions, asked only after the view is pinned down, followed by silence. The coach marks *wisdom* down if it comes too early or the trainee answers it himself. The opponent must answer as real people do: concede, dodge, or change the subject. Never invent an escape the bank doesn't record.

**The bridge to Christ (SOP Section A′).** Drilled only in Stage 10, as in the shared rules in Training Plans. The bridge question is: "If God forbids the bow to images, where would you look to see Christ?"

**After passing.** Run one full conversation before the Sunday of Orthodoxy (the first Sunday of Lent), when Orthodox churches celebrate icons, and add every new response you meet to the bank.
