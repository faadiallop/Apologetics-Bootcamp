**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** In Stage 1 the trainee maps every answer to each question in the SOP's "Exposing the flaws" table and what it costs. In Stage 6, and from Stage 8 on, every round must end with one of those questions, asked only after the view is pinned down, followed by silence. The coach marks *wisdom* down if it comes too early or the trainee answers it himself. The opponent must answer as real people do: concede, dodge, or change the subject. Never invent an escape the bank doesn't record.

**The bridge to Christ (SOP Section A′).** This is drilled only in Stage 9, after the method is passed, so that he learns one thing at a time. Until then a round ends when the opponent answers the exposing question, and the coach neither asks for, hints at nor scores the bridge or the gospel; if the trainee crosses before the round's goal is met, the opponent answers in a sentence and the round closes. Each round still ends with his closing turns, his chance to point to Christ in a sentence (step 3 of a round, above). In Stage 9 he first tells the gospel aloud with no opponent: who Jesus is, our sin, his death for sinners and his rising, and the call to repent and believe, true to Scripture and in his own words (references not required). Then each round opens just after the exposing question, and the opponent either gives the costly answer or dodges; the coach varies it. The trainee crosses to Christ within two turns: after a concession, with the SOP's bridge question; after a dodge, by parking the point and giving the reason for his hope. When he asks leave to tell the gospel, the member gives him room or closes the door, and the coach varies this too ("Go on, I'm listening," "So what would I have to do?", "I've got to run"). The coach marks *wisdom* down if he keeps arguing instead of crossing, or presses the gospel after the door has closed, and *knowledge* down if what he says of it is false or would mislead, or if he had room and left out who Jesus is, sin, the cross and resurrection, or the call to repent and believe. A short word that fits a closing door, with a Gospel left behind, loses nothing. Pass: crossed within two turns, 4 of 5 rounds.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation before each Memorial season, when Witnesses invite the public, and add every new response you meet to the bank.
