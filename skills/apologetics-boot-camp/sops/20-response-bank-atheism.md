## Borrowed Ground: questions and statements from debates

What Christian debaters have actually asked or said to atheists in public debates, for coaches to model and trainees to practice. Use them inside the SOP's question plan, one at a time, and listen to the answer. A line that works before an audience can sound like a gotcha across a table; ask to understand (Prov. 18:13), and keep any sharp word brief (presupposition-rule 6).

The 2023 Utah lines come from Durbin's breakdown of his cross-examination of Anderson, whose transcript also has no speaker labels; the debate's own captions, and Chatterjee's cross-examination, have not been gathered.
