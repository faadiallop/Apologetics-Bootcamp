## Training plan: The Suffering Servant

Source SOP: Jewish people. Bank rows refer to the Suffering Servant table in the Response Bank.

| Stage | Skill | Drill | Opponent | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; why Rom. 1:16 and Luke 24:26; the Targum and Sanhedrin 98b; why we avoid "forbidden chapter" | None | What the passages say, and other facts, right in substance |
| 2. Premises | Knowledge | Learn, then recite the three premises, two supporting verses each, from memory | None | Two supporting passages for every point, said correctly (references not required) |
| 3. Gather | Columbo step 1 (ch. 4) | "Have you read Isaiah 53? Who is it about? Who taught you that?" Questions only, four turns | Secular Jew | Enough questions to pin down the view; no statements; its core restated in the trainee's own words and confirmed by the member |
| 4. Burden of proof | Columbo step 2 (ch. 5) | "What in the text shows the servant is Israel?" | Traditional Jew; rows 1, 2, 8 | Burden stays on the claimant every time, and he asks for the reasons behind each main claim (not every passing remark) |

When the talk turns to Christian persecution, or to why Jesus didn't bring peace (SOP B′), he honors it, parks it, and returns to Isaiah 53 | Counter-missionary student; Traditional Jew | Composure kept; no burden taken on; back on Isaiah 53 within two turns, by the way back that fits |
| 6. Steamroller | Steamroller (ch. 14) | Opponent fires Hebrew points and interrupts, three times; trainee stops him, shames him gently, then leaves with no jab, door open, and an offer to read Isaiah 53 together another time | Counter-missionary student steamrolling | Each step at its own interruption, none early or skipped; calm throughout; the exit names no motive and leaves the door open |

| 11. Full conversations | All | About 15 turns, opener to A′ (read Luke 24 or Acts 8 together) | Secular Jew, then Traditional Jew, then Counter-missionary student | Pass on the rubric with each persona, in order; he meets the next only after passing the one before. After the third, he is street ready |

**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** Each question has a recorded escape, listed in the SOP. In Stage 7, and from Stage 9 on, every round ends with one of those questions, then silence; the opponent uses the recorded escape the first time, and the trainee must answer it with the follow-up question.

**The bridge to Christ (SOP Section A′).** This is drilled only in Stage 10, after the method is passed, so that he learns one thing at a time. Until then a round ends when the opponent answers the exposing question, and the coach neither asks for, hints at nor scores the bridge or the gospel; if the trainee crosses before the round's goal is met, the opponent answers in a sentence and the round closes. Each round still ends with his closing turns, his chance to point to Christ in a sentence (step 3 of a round, above). In Stage 10 he first tells the gospel aloud with no opponent: who Jesus is, our sin, his death for sinners and his rising, and the call to repent and believe, true to Scripture and in his own words (references not required). Then each round opens just after the exposing question, and the opponent either gives the costly answer or dodges; the coach varies it. The trainee crosses to Christ within two turns: after a concession, with the SOP's bridge question; after a dodge, by parking the point and giving the reason for his hope. When he asks leave to tell the gospel, the member gives him room or closes the door, and the coach varies this too ("Go on, I'm listening," "So what would I have to do?", "I've got to run"). The coach marks *wisdom* down if he keeps arguing instead of crossing, or presses the gospel after the door has closed, and *knowledge* down if what he says of it is false or would mislead, or if he had room and left out who Jesus is, sin, the cross and resurrection, or the call to repent and believe. A short word that fits a closing door, with a Gospel left behind, loses nothing. Pass: crossed within two turns, 4 of 5 rounds.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation a month with a random persona, and add every new response you meet to the bank.
