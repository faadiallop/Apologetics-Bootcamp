## Training plan: Icon Veneration

Source SOP: Icon Veneration. Bank rows refer to the Icon Veneration table in the Response Bank. The trainee chooses whether to train for Orthodox Christians or for Catholics first; the argument is the same, and the Cradle persona and the council quoted (Nicaea II or Trent) follow his choice.

Foundation | Knowledge | Coach teaches, then checks: the argument in one sentence; the terms (*proskynesis*, *latreia* or *latria*, *dulia*, prototype); what Nicaea II and Trent decreed (a kiss, a bow, incense and lights, an anathema); that Catholics number the commandment differently | None | What the passages say right in substance; terms rightly defined |

After the third, he is street ready |

**Exposing the flaws (SOP Section C).** In Stage 1 the trainee maps every answer to each question in the SOP's "Exposing the flaws" table and what it costs. In Stage 7, and from Stage 9 on, every round must end with one of those questions, asked only after the view is pinned down, followed by silence. The coach marks *wisdom* down if it comes too early or the trainee answers it himself. The opponent must answer as real people do: concede, dodge, or change the subject. Never invent an escape the bank doesn't record.

**The bridge to Christ (SOP Section A′).** Drilled only in Stage 10, as in the shared rules in Training Plans. The bridge question is: "If God forbids the bow to images, where would you look to see Christ?" Two samples for a missed closing turn, each built from the member's own words: to "Christ was seen and touched," "Could we read together what the men who touched him wrote?"; to "I'm honoring Mary," "Could we read what she said about her Son?" She said, "Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it" (John 2:5). After an argument about images, a statement of where we see Christ reads as a contrast with his icons; an invitation to read does not.

**After passing.** Run one full conversation before the Sunday of Orthodoxy (the first Sunday of Lent), when Orthodox churches celebrate icons, and before Good Friday, when Catholics venerate the cross. Add every new response you meet to the bank. For practice full conversations, when he asks for them, vary the persona among all the group's personas, these included: the other cradle persona, Oriental Orthodox, Eastern Catholic, Anglo-Catholic.
