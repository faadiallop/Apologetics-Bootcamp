| # | They say | Where seen | Reply and tactic |

| 1 | "Only those in the new covenant partake. The other sheep aren't in it." | [jw.org](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/502015257); [w88](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1988090) | The covenant promises forgiveness to its members, "from the least to the greatest" (Heb. 8:11–12). "Where are you forgiven if not in it?" |
| 2 | "We aren't in the covenant, but we're still forgiven by its blood. | 3 | "John 6:53 wasn't said in connection with the Memorial." | [Reasoning book](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1101989248); [w24 December](https://www.jw.org/en/library/magazines/watchtower-study-december-2024/Everlasting-Life-for-You-But-How) | Your literature grants John 6's life is for everyone. | 4 | "The other sheep aren't in union with Jesus as members of his body." | [w52 2/15](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1952122) | John 6:56 says he that eateth "dwelleth in me, and I in him," and 6:53 says without eating there is no life. "Then where is your life?" (Taking the Roof Off) |
| 5 | "Jesus was teaching with a metaphor." | [jw.org](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/502015257) | Agreed. A metaphor for what *all* must do to live (John 6:53). |
| 6 | "There's a little flock and other sheep: two groups." | [w10 3/15](https://www.jw.org/ase/library/magazines/w20100315/One-Flock-One-Shepherd/); Luke 12:32 | John 10:16 says they become "one fold, and one shepherd." |
| 7 | "The covenant for a kingdom (Luke 22:28–30) is for those who will rule." | [Reasoning book](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1101989248) | All believers are made "kings and priests unto God" (Rev. 1:6), and all partake of one bread (1 Cor. 10:17). |
| 8 | "Partaking unworthily brings judgment." | [w53](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1953167) | "Let a man examine himself, and so let him eat" (1 Cor. 11:28). See SOP B′. |

| 10 | "Anointing depends on God, not on the one wishing" (Rom. 9:16). | [w91](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1991204) | So does all mercy, and he promises it to everyone who calls (Rom. 10:13). |
| 11 | "We attend to give thanks for the ransom (1 John 2:2)." | [jw.org](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/502015257) | Jesus said "this do," not "watch this" (Luke 22:19). |
| 12 | "More partakers is no cause for concern; some are mistaken or have emotional problems." | [w96](https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1996607); [w20 January](https://www.jw.org/en/library/magazines/watchtower-study-january-2020/we-will-go-with-you/) | "What result would ever count against the teaching?" (Unfalsifiable claim) |

| 16 | Of the great crowd of Rev. 7:9: they stand *before* the throne, not in heaven. "If you're standing before McDonald's, right, that means you're not in it." | An inactive Witness on a Christian host's livestream, undated, in substance ([~39:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FmiVGmBDqOQ&t=2387s)). | 17 | *Answer to an invitation:* No, thank you. (Offered a tract, a book or a Gospel.)
